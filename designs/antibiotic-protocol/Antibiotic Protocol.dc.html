Hip & Knee Joint Replacement

Antibiotic Protocol for Dental Work

If you have a joint replacement, we recommend that you be pre-medicated with antibiotics before any dental appointment for the first 2 years after your surgery date. Bacteria in the mouth can travel directly into the bloodstream and infect the joint, putting you at an increased risk of joint infection.

If you have already had surgery, wait at least 6 months after surgery before any dental work. If your dental procedure is an emergency, understand that there is still a risk of infection.

RECOMMENDED PRE-MEDICATION
Routine cleaning
Amoxicillin 500 mg — take 4 tablets (2 grams) one hour prior to your dental appointment.
Allergic to penicillin? Clindamycin 300 mg with the same directions.
Dental procedures & surgeries
Keflex 500 mg — take 1 tablet twice daily, starting 2 days prior to your dental appointment (#10 tablets), until finished.
Allergic to penicillin? Clindamycin 300 mg with the same directions.
Please note
• Cleanings and dental work are allowed starting 6 months post-op. • Should you decide not to proceed with our protocol, it is at your own risk. • Questions? Call us at 346-571-7500 or message us through KLARA.
